# 2026.245.1259 — fix(vm): OMARCHY_PATH on the channel keeper/twin steps

## Summary of changes

Fixes a real runtime defect surfaced by the first live anchor run of the channel-twin family (`check-charly-omarchy-rc-vm`, calver 2026.245.1246): `/usr/bin/omarchy-channel-set` exits 1 with **`OMARCHY_PATH: unbound variable`** (line 75) when the env var is unset, failing the `channel-roundtrip-*` checks. Like `omarchy update` and `omarchy-migrate`, `omarchy-channel-set` requires `OMARCHY_PATH`.

- Adds `env: {OMARCHY_PATH: /usr/share/omarchy}` (the installed-tree path) to every `channel-switch-*`, `channel-update-*` and `channel-roundtrip-*` step in the three keepers and three twins (18 steps).
- The anchor run passed everything else in the entity family (full charly/toolchain suite, `omarchy-autologin-configured`, `charly-omarchy-cli-present`, 9/14), and the golden-snapshot step (not reached because the bed stops at the failed check) follows this fix.
